# Privacy Policy for Yearly

**Effective Date:** October 5, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 5, 2026

At Yearly ("we", "our", or "us"), we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Yearly is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy and protect your personal information.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
Yearly does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, location data, device identifiers, or usage analytics. 

### 2. 100% On-Device Processing
All computations—including tracking elapsed days, calculating percentages, and rendering the calendar dot lattice—take place entirely on your device using your system's native calendar and time settings. No data ever leaves your device.

### 3. No Third-Party Trackers or Analytics
Yearly does not contain any third-party SDKs, tracking frameworks, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or analytics tools.

### 4. System Permissions
Yearly requires **no permissions** to operate. It does not access your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, location, or health data.

### 5. Home Screen & Lock Screen Widgets
The Yearly widget operates completely offline and reads only your device's current date and your appearance preference (Light, Dark, or System) to render your progress at a glance.

### 6. Children's Privacy
Because Yearly collects no information whatsoever, we comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### 7. Changes to This Policy
If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date.
