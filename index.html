Privacy Policy for Yearly

Effective Date: October 5, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Summary & Core Promise

Yearly collects zero data. We believe your calendar and your time belong solely to you. Yearly operates 100% on-device, requires no account, connects to no third-party servers, and contains no advertising or tracking software.

2. No Personal Information Collected

When you use Yearly, we do not collect, process, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Your location or IP address
  • Your device identifiers or Advertising Identifier (IDFA)
  • Your usage habits or in-app activity

3. 100% On-Device Calculations

All calculations—such as days elapsed, days remaining, and percentage completed—are computed in real time on your device using Apple's standard system Calendar APIs. No dates or timestamps are ever transmitted over the internet.

4. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Analytics

Yearly does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or social media tracking tools. The app is completely self-contained.

5. Device Permissions

Yearly requests no permissions. It does not access your Camera, Microphone, Photos, Contacts, Health data, or Location.

6. Home Screen & Lock Screen Widgets

The Yearly widget reads your device's current date and your appearance preference (Light, Dark, or System) locally. It schedules updates at 12:00 AM midnight locally through iOS WidgetKit without network communication.

7. Children's Privacy

Because Yearly does not collect any personal data from anyone, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
